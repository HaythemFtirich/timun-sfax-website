TIMUN Sfax — motion direction

Interaction thesis: TIMUN Sfax will feel like one considered descent through an institutional record: a 900ms line-by-line hero reveal with a 120ms step sets the pace, scroll drives 1.02→1.08 image push-in and ±80px layered drift inside sticky chapters, 720ms intersection reveals arrive on a 70ms stagger capped at 420ms, and a 180ms hover language keeps controls quiet — all transform/opacity-only in a single rAF pass, with a static, fully readable state under reduced motion or a slow connection.

Allowed patterns: full-viewport hero · canvas-measured display lines · sticky chapter stages · hairline gold progress rail · thin rules · mono micro-labels · scroll cue fading at 48px · single warm-gold accent on navy · generous negative space · odometer figures only for approved statistics · reduced-motion static fallback.

This page is the proposal, not the build. It shows the exact timing and curve; nothing here becomes implementation code.
